## 💻 3. Landing Page Design Options (Slides 1 & 2)

The deck presents **4 distinct UI mockup concepts** for the SlideBee website:

### Option 1: Dark Obsidian Hero with Template Floating Previews
* **Headline:** *"Stunning Slides. Made Easy."*
* **Subhead:** *"Explore thousands of creative PowerPoint templates to make your presentations stand out."*
* **Key Features:** Centered Search bar with yellow button (`Search for templates...`), floating 3D deck preview cards (Business Presentation, Marketing Strategy, Company Profile), and a Category icon strip (Business, Marketing, Education, Sales, Medical, Technology, Finance).
* **Trust Badges:** `10,000+ Templates` | `Fully Editable` | `Easy to Customize` | `Premium Quality`

### Option 2: Clean Light & Yellow Dual-Tone Layout
* **Headline:** *"Create. Present. Impress."*
* **Subhead:** *"Save time and create amazing presentations with our creative templates."*
* **Layout:** Light warm background with a split hero showing laptop mockup + floating slide editor UI.
* **Feature Row:** 4 Yellow Banner Cards:
  * 🛡️ **Easy to Customize** (Fully editable slides with placeholders)
  * 📐 **Modern & Unique** (Professional designs for every need)
  * 🔄 **Lifetime Updates** (Free updates for lifetime)
  * 🎧 **Premium Support** (We're here to help anytime)

### Option 3: Honeycomb Bee-Themed Creative Deck
* **Headline:** *"Buzzing with Creative Slides — Find the Perfect Template For Every Presentation"*
* **Visual:** Floating hexagonal honeycomb cluster of slide previews (Business Plan, Startup Pitch Deck, Marketing Strategy, Project Proposal).

### Option 4: Corporate Executive Dark Mode
* **Headline:** *"Your Next Presentation Starts Here."*
* **Subhead:** *"Explore high-quality templates crafted to make your ideas shine."*
* **Feature Grid:** *"Why Choose Slidebee?"* (Premium Quality, Time Saving, Easy Editing, Affordable Pricing).
